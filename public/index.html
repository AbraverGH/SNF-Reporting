Month — pick one or more, each year+month independent

Days, average daily rate & PPD by payer

Uses the Facilities, Payer Type, and Year/Month filters above. Click an HMO or Unmapped row to see the individual payors rolled into it, with their own rate and level detail — useful for figuring out exactly which payors need to be added to the Payer Mapping.

Avg Daily Rate is the gross/billed rate per day (Gross ÷ Days) — the contracted rate before any deductions. PPD is net revenue per day (Net ÷ Days) — what's actually realized after coinsurance and other deductions come out. The two only match when a payer has no deductions that period; otherwise PPD will run lower than Avg Daily Rate.

PPD trend — month over month

One line per payer (the rows currently shown on the Summary tab), so you can compare how rates are trending across months.

Average Daily Rate by month — by payer

Gross rate per day (before deductions), by payer, broken out by month.

PPD by month — by payer

Net revenue per patient day, by payer, broken out by month.

Days by level

Skilled payers only — total days, average daily rate, and PPD per rate/level, combined across every selected payer except NE Medicaid and Managed Medicaid, which each have their own tab. ALF-only rates (Monthly Rate, Rural Single, Daily Rate) are excluded from all of Skilled Analysis, since those aren't skilled nursing levels. Bariatric and Dialysis variants (different ICD codes, "Rate" vs not, etc.) are grouped into one row apiece.

Days by level — by month

Same rows as above (Skilled payers only, NE Medicaid and Managed Medicaid excluded), broken out by month instead of totaled together.

Level detail — by payer

Click any payer to see the rate/level breakdown underneath it (Level 1, Level 2, Bariatric, Trach, etc.), pulled from the specific rate code billed on each transaction. Codes that don't resolve to a named level — mostly PDPM-billed stays, which use case-mix groups rather than named levels — fall into "(No rate detail)" rather than being dropped.

Level detail — by facility

Same breakdown as above, grouped by facility instead of payer — click any facility to see its rate/level mix underneath it.

NE Medicaid — days by level

NE Medicaid pays on its own rate structure — the only Medicaid payer that does, so it's broken out here rather than being mixed into the rest of Skilled Analysis. ALF-only rates (Monthly Rate, Rural Single, Daily Rate) are excluded from all of Skilled Analysis, since those aren't skilled nursing levels. Uses the same Facilities and Year/Month filters as the other tabs.

AR Collections — PCC (Paramount)

For facilities billing out of PointClickCare instead of the primary system. Journal Entries covers Revenue and Cash Receipts (both split automatically by facility — ALF/ILF/SNF); the AR Aging Report covers Aging Balance. Both are combined into the AR Collections sources above automatically, alongside the other facilities' own data.

Medicare Days — PCC (Paramount)

A focused "A/R - Medicare A" export from PointClickCare, covering Paramount SNF's traditional Medicare activity — separate from the shared Sales Journal, so it doesn't affect Skilled Analysis' view of this facility (which would need a full Sales Journal, not a Medicare-only one). Feeds Medicare Days directly, alongside the other facilities' own Sales Journal data.
